THE BUILD PLAN

Your forms.
Your brand.
Your own workspace.

A focused first version, shaped around how you actually test and report.

01 / FIRST WORKING REPORT

Start with your biosafety cabinet form.

Agree the fields, calculation methods, report layout and review steps using your existing report and blank source worksheet.

02 / MATCH THE JOB

Add the equipment-specific variants.

Confirm the single-zone, multi-zone and fume-hood forms, then map each to its own fields and report wording.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Private access, under your domain.

Plan a secure reporting area alongside your GoDaddy website, with saved records and PDF output. Review approved procedure versions and any compliance requirements before live use.

04 / LEARN AS WE BUILD

Know how to manage it yourself.

A guided handover can cover editing wording, managing forms and making future changes. Final scope and hours are agreed after reviewing the remaining templates.
