Good afternoon, Dr. Berger’s practice. How can I help?
Hi, I’m a new patient. Part of my front tooth broke yesterday and I’d like someone to look at it.
Of course. Are you currently experiencing severe pain, swelling, or bleeding?
No, luckily not.
Understood. I can help you arrange an appointment. We have availability tomorrow at 11:20 or 15:40. Would either work?
15:40 would be perfect.
Done. I’ve booked you for tomorrow at 15:40 and noted that you’re a new patient with a chipped front tooth. I’ll send the appointment confirmation shortly. Is there anything else you’d like the practice to know?
No, that’s all.
Perfect. We’ll see you tomorrow at 15:40.

The Problem

Every unanswered call is a missed opportunity

Calls arrive while your team is busy, after you’ve closed, or when nobody is available to pick up. Some of those callers won’t try again.

Missed calls

Customers call while your team is already busy.

After-hours

New inquiries don’t stop when the working day does.

Repetitive requests

Staff repeatedly answer the same questions and scheduling requests.

Inconsistent intake

Important caller details can get lost between the phone call and the next action.

What It Does

A receptionist for the phone, built around your business

Available actions depend on how your receptionist is configured and which systems are connected.

Answers & understands

Answers naturally and identifies why the customer is calling.

Handles routine requests

Provides approved information about services, hours, availability, and common questions.

Captures what matters

Collects the details your team needs before the conversation ends.

Books appointments

Checks availability and schedules according to your booking rules.

Routes when needed

Transfers or escalates calls according to rules you define.

Keeps your team informed

Creates a structured record of what happened and what happens next.
