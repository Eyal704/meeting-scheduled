Private jets in Austria: who to contact first

For Shimi at fleckweg · 30 people, strongest first · click a person to see the first email

How the list was built

  • Jet owners come from the Austrian aircraft register, as matched in a 2023 Greenpeace analysis, and from press reports.
  • Most jets are held through management companies, so the list leads with the people who run those fleets and the two private jet terminals at Vienna Airport.
  • Vienna comes first, since fleckweg works there. Salzburg, Linz, Innsbruck and Klagenfurt follow.

How the emails are written

  • Each one opens with something real about that person or company.
  • They offer what fleckweg does well: deep cleaning of seats, sofas, carpets and bed mattresses in the cabin, done on site, using the cleaning products each aircraft maker approves.
  • Written in the language each person is most at home in, with an English translation where needed. Short, plain, and one small ask.
