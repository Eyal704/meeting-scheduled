How it works

Three simple steps.

The goal is not to give you another outbound control panel to babysit. The goal is to get outbound moving, keep it relevant, and pull you in when a conversation is worth your time.

01

Connect your inbox and define your customer

Connect Gmail, describe what you sell, and define who a good fit looks like. No heavy setup, no complex workflow builder.

02

The agent finds who to contact and why

MeetingScheduled maps accounts, finds ICP contacts, watches signals, and starts personalized outreach based on what is relevant right now.

03

You focus on replies and calls

When someone responds, the agent prepares the conversation — context, scripts, and next steps — so you spend your time talking, not hunting.
