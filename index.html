Why teams switch

Outbound breaks in the same four places every time.

01

Setup takes too long

Most platforms start with a setup project, not a result. Sequence builders, list imports, persona configs — by the time it's running, you're a project manager, not a salesperson.

02

Prospecting is too static

The right prospect isn't just a profile. It's someone specific, at a specific moment, in a specific situation. Most tools help you find the profile. Few of them turn live momentum into sent outreach.

03

Reps do the wrong work

Research, tab-switching, drafting, sequence management — hours that should go to conversations and calls go to prep instead.

04

The outreach itself is weak

Even "personalized" emails read as shallow and automated. Poor outreach doesn't just get ignored — it damages your respect and brand.
