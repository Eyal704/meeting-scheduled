From a business goal to rollout

Operations, IT and management get a shared view of the requirements before committing to development.

  1. Goals and requirements

    Agree with the people responsible on what should improve and how to tell — available service capacity, reports returned for correction or visibility into outstanding invoices.

  2. System design and proposal

    Map core systems, permissions and exceptions. The proposal defines deliverables, integrations, costs and acceptance criteria.

  3. Development and rollout

    Users test scenarios from their own work. Rollout planning covers business continuity, training and exception handling.

  4. Handover and support

    Agree who owns support, how the company can access its data and how changes will be handled. Review performance against the agreed goals after rollout.

How will the system fit your organisation?

Which records stay in our ERP or CRM?

With IT, establish which system owns each record, who may update it and how failed updates are handled. Integration feasibility is checked before a commitment is made.

Where does AI make sense?

For example, AI can turn a dictated service note into a draft visit summary. Retain the source for review and define who approves the summary before it joins the asset history.

What happens at handover?

The proposal sets out deliverables, documentation, training and support terms. It also defines data export, ongoing maintenance and how future changes are handled.
