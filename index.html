Workflows end to end

From the moment work arrives to the moment it's done

Every system we build follows the same shape: it starts when something real happens, gathers the context a good employee would gather, prepares the work, and stops for a person wherever judgement matters.

Field service

A periodic test, from the visit to the signed report

  1. Trigger

    A test is due at a customer site.

  2. Context

    The customer, the site, the equipment and last year’s results are already on the technician’s phone.

  3. Draft

    The technician enters readings and photos, and a report draft is laid out in your format.

  4. Your team

    The qualified reviewer approves the report or sends it back with a note.

  5. Outcome

    The customer gets the report, and the unit’s history and next due date are updated.

Aftermarket

A quiet customer, back to a quote request

  1. Trigger

    A customer who ordered parts every year stops ordering.

  2. Context

    Order history, installed machines and past contacts are reviewed.

  3. Draft

    A short message about their machine is prepared, with one easy question.

  4. Your team

    Your team approves the account and the wording before anything is sent.

  5. Outcome

    The customer’s need reaches your parts or service team, ready to quote.

Custom system · sales engineering

Quoting a technical requirement

  1. Trigger

    A customer sends technical requirements by email.

  2. Context

    Specifications, past quotes, supplier documents, and pricing rules are pulled together.

  3. Draft

    A structured brief and a recommended configuration are written, with assumptions listed.

  4. Your team

    An engineer checks the assumptions, adjusts, and approves.

  5. Outcome

    The proposal goes out and the CRM and quote record are updated.

What changes from company to company is the trigger, the context that matters and who signs off. That is exactly the part we design around how your business actually works.

How we work

Built around your process, not around a demo

The hard part is rarely the model. It's knowing which workflow is worth the effort, how the work really gets done, and where the information actually lives. That understanding is part of what we deliver.

1

Find the right workflow

We look for work that happens often, costs skilled people hours, and has a clear finish line. One workflow, bounded, worth doing.

2

Design it around the real thing

We sit with the people who do the work today — the exceptions, the judgement calls, the steps nobody wrote down — and design around that.

3

Connect the systems

Email, phone, documents, CRM, ERP, and internal knowledge. The workflow reads from where your information already lives and writes back to it.

4

Deploy, measure, improve

We put it in front of real work, watch what it gets wrong, and tighten it. Then we look at the next workflow with everything we learned.

What stays true in every system

A system only gets used if the people who own the work trust it. These are the commitments behind every system we put into production.

Works with what you already run

You don't need to replace the systems your team already uses. We connect to your CRM, ERP, phone system, inboxes, and shared drives.

People make the decisions that matter

Wherever professional judgement or accountability matters, the work stops for a human to review it. Your experts stay the experts.

Start with one, expand on evidence

We begin with a single bounded workflow you can judge honestly. What comes next is decided by what that one actually delivered.
