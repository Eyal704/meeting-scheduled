MeetingScheduled is an AI-powered B2B outbound platform. It helps sales teams, agencies, and founders launch high-quality outbound in 1 minute, find prospects through live signals and real-time triggers, reduce manual SDR/AE work by 80%, and generate outreach that performs at a 90+ quality standard.

This is not a sequencing tool. This is not a template blaster. This is a full outbound system — research, signal scoring, draft generation, sequence management, deliverability — in one platform.

One-liner: MeetingScheduled gets B2B teams live in 1 minute, prospects through real-time signals, and generates outreach that actually gets replies.

## What We Lead With

Always open with a signal — something that tells us this person is actively dealing with the problem we solve. Strong triggers:

- They're hiring SDRs or BDRs (they're scaling outbound manually → we can help them do it with fewer people or get more out of the ones they have)
- They're hiring a Head of Sales or VP of Sales (they're formalizing a go-to-market motion)
- They posted or engaged with content about cold email, outbound, pipeline generation, or SDR productivity
- They recently joined a company in a sales leadership role (new to the seat, looking to make an impact fast)
- Their job description for reps mentions research, prospecting, personalization — signals they're doing this manually
- They're a known user of Clay, Apollo, or Lemlist (they're already investing in outbound infrastructure)
- Their company just raised funding (they're about to scale a sales team)

## Competitive Positioning

**vs. Apollo / Instantly / Lemlist:** Those are sender/sequence tools. They don't research. They don't score signals. They don't gate email quality. They automate volume. We automate competence.

**vs. Clay:** Clay is a powerful data infrastructure tool. It requires significant setup, technical skill, and toolchain assembly. We're the complete motion — research, signal scoring, drafting, sequencing, sending — in one system, ready in 1 minute.

**vs. hiring more SDRs:** One rep with MeetingScheduled covers the output of a team doing things manually, at a fraction of the cost, with more consistent quality.

**Our unfair advantage:** Built by someone who actually worked the job. The system reflects the judgment of an experienced SDR, not a product team guessing at the workflow.

## Tone and Voice for Outreach

- Direct. No fluff. No filler intros.
- Commercially aware — talk about pipeline, reply rates, rep time, unit economics. Not "AI" as a buzzword.
- Confident but not arrogant. We have something real. We don't need to over-claim.
- No: "I hope this finds you well." No: "I wanted to reach out." No: "Our AI-powered platform leverages..."
- Lead with their world (the signal, the trigger, the pain) before introducing ours.
- One clear, low-friction CTA. Usually: quick call or a question that earns a reply.

---

## What NOT to Say

- Do not claim we integrate with every tool in their stack unless confirmed
- Do not promise specific reply rate numbers (we improve quality; results vary by ICP and offer)
- Do not use "revolutionary," "game-changing," "disruptive," or other empty superlatives
- Do not lead with features — lead with their problem
- Do not mention the technical stack (Next.js, Supabase, BullMQ) in prospect-facing copy
