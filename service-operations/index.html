Service operations

Service operations from dispatch to visit closure

As service volumes grow, dispatchers, technicians and service managers need the same equipment history. A service system connects the call, the work performed and the next office decision.

Demonstration scenario

A second technician visits the same machine

In this service demonstration, a customer reports a recurring fault. Dispatch needs to assign the visit and the next technician needs to know what was already tried.

  1. Before the visit

    Dispatch checks the asset record and previous visits, then schedules the job around visit duration and travel time.

  2. On site

    The technician opens the job and records hours, parts, photos and any outstanding work.

  3. In the office

    The service lead reviews the record, requests missing details or passes the visit on for a billing check or repeat appointment.

Questions to settle before development

We already have field software. What could change?

Look at the handoffs. If field reporting works well, the gap may be in how the office opens a job, transfers responsibility or checks billable items.

How would this connect to Priority or another ERP?

We agree with your IT team on what information the systems will share. Once they give us access, we connect the two systems.

How would we assess the impact?

Track visits waiting for closure, office time spent resolving missing information and parts or hours awaiting a billing review.
