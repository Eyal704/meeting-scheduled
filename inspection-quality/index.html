Demonstration scenario

A reading needs a second look

In the public sprinkler inspection demo, switch between the inspector and the office to see how a correction is recorded before sign-off.

  1. Capture the evidence

    The inspector records readings and findings in the context of the equipment and the inspection.

  2. Review in the office

    The reviewer compares the draft with the source readings, records the question and sends the report back for clarification.

  3. Correct and approve

    The inspector records the correction and the reason. The reviewer checks the change and decides whether to approve the report.

Questions to settle before development

What if we have dozens of report formats?

Map what varies by customer, test type and form version. Use representative reports to validate conditional fields, calculations and photo layouts.

Who judges whether a finding is acceptable?

Your organisation defines who can assess a finding, return it for clarification and sign off. Software or AI can help prepare a draft where requirements and accuracy checks support its use.

How would we test the fit?

Measure write-up time, reasons for returns and the extent of corrections. Compare outputs against client requirements, including spreadsheet calculations and export format.
