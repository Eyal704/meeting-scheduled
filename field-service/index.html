    Before you send a form

    We already have inspection software.

    We look at what works and what still happens outside it. The first question is whether a focused change or connection can close that gap. Specific integrations are confirmed during scoping.

    Will our technicians actually use it?

    Have a technician and a reviewer try the example on the same job. We check the field order, entry effort and missing information before expanding to other forms.

    What about our Excel calculations?

    We map the spreadsheets, calculations and review controls you rely on. Any changes or connections need to be defined and checked with your technical team.

    Does the system approve the inspection?

    No. It prepares the report from recorded data. Technical judgment and approval stay with the people you designate. The sample is not a complete inspection form or a claim of regulatory acceptance.

    What does it cost?

    The one-form example is free. The production price depends on the forms, workflow and integrations. You receive the scope and price in writing before committing.

    What happens after launch?

    The agreement sets out support, fixes, changes, data export and what happens if we stop working together. These details are agreed before production use.

    What happens to the files I upload?

    Send blank or redacted documents where possible. I use the files to prepare your example and will delete them on request. Before using live customer data in a production system, we agree access, storage and retention.

    Can technicians work without a connection?

    If that matters at your sites, include it when we scope the system. Offline capture and synchronization need to be designed and tested; this browser demo does not demonstrate them.
