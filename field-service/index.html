What owners tell me

The test takes an hour. The report takes the rest of the week.

When I sit with owners of testing and service companies, the same three things come up. None of them is about the technical work.

01

The same details, every visit

The customer, the site and the equipment haven’t changed since last year, yet the technician writes them out again on every form.

02

Every report is written twice

The paper form comes back to the office. Someone retypes it into Excel or Word and digs the customer’s history out of folders. One report can take hours, and what the customer finally gets is often a dense Excel sheet that is hard to read.

03

Old reports are hard to find

A customer has an audit and needs last year’s certificate today. Who is due for a test this month is worked out by hand, month after month.

If one of these sounds like your week, the system below is built to remove it.

    Common questions

    We already have a system.

    Good. What does it do well, and what is still done by hand? If reports are still retyped or history still lives in folders, that part can be built next to what you have, not instead of it.

    Will the technicians actually use it?

    The form on the phone follows the paper form they already know, with the repeated details already filled in. It should be faster for them than paper, and the example lets them try it before anything is built.

    How soon do we see the example?

    Usually within a few working days of receiving your form. If your form is long or unusual, I’ll tell you when to expect it.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on how many forms and steps you have. After I’ve seen one form, I can give you a real number instead of a guess, with a fixed scope in writing.

    Where does AI come in?

    Where it saves time and a person still checks the result: for example, turning a technician’s short notes into clean report text. The readings, the pass or fail, and the signature stay exactly as entered and approved.

    What happens after it goes live?

    Support, fixes and changes are part of the written agreement: what’s included, how quickly problems are handled, and how new forms are added. You won’t be left with a system nobody looks after.

    What happens to our customer data?

    For the example, only the form you choose to send. Before any real customer data is involved, we agree in writing what is stored, where, who can access it, and what happens if we stop working together.
