How a client uses the app

  1. Review the plan. The Today screen shows the next workout and the weekly schedule.
  2. Record the workout. In Train, the client opens an exercise and logs the reps and weight for each set.
  3. Review progress. Saved workouts appear in Progress, with session history and exercise comparisons.
  4. Prepare a check-in. In Steven, the client records energy, confidence, a win and anything they need help with. They can review a weekly report before sharing it by email. This screen also links to Steven’s existing content.
